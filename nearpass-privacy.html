Privacy Policy — Nearpass

App: Nearpass (Android) — published by Desho.Co.Ltd

Effective Date: October 2026

Last Updated: October 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Nearpass moves files between devices that are physically near each other. Everything is sent directly from your device to the other device over Wi-Fi or a local network. There is no Nearpass server, no account, no upload and no cloud storage — your files never leave the two phones that are talking to each other.

This Privacy Policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

2. What Nearpass Does Not Collect

  • We do not collect, transmit or store your files. Transfers are peer-to-peer and end on the receiving device.
  • No name, email, phone number, address or account is requested or stored.
  • No location permission is requested. The Wi-Fi and Bluetooth permissions the app uses are declared neverForLocation and are used only to find and connect to a nearby device.
  • No contacts, calendar, camera, microphone or call-log access.
  • No analytics, no usage profiling, no remote crash reports by default.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

3. Data Stored On Your Device

  • Transfer history and settings: names of files you have sent or received, transfer results and your preferences are kept in the app's private storage so you can see what happened on this device.
  • Benchmark results: the diagnostic tool writes a small CSV of speed measurements into the app's own storage folder. It contains sizes and timings, not file contents.
  • Files you choose to receive: saved where you tell the app to save them, in your gallery or folder, and visible only on that device.

All of it is deleted when you uninstall the app, and you can clear it sooner: Settings > Apps > Nearpass > Storage > Clear data.

4. What Is Sent Between Devices

To connect two phones, the app exchanges a short-lived connection identifier and the device name you see on the other screen. To transfer, it sends the file bytes you selected. This traffic stays on the local link between the two devices; it is not routed through us and we cannot see it.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Nearpass is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads appear at the end of a transfer session and in the file list, never during a moving transfer.

  • Advertising ID (AAID) and device signals: AdMob may use your device advertising identifier, device model, OS version and app information to serve and measure ads. This is the only data the app passes to a third party, and it is passed by Google's SDK, not by our code.
  • Ad interactions: impressions, clicks and completions may be recorded by AdMob and its advertisers.
  • Consent: before the first ad, Google's User Messaging Platform may ask you to accept or decline personalised ads in regions where that is required. You can change that choice at any time through "Ad privacy settings" in the app, which opens Google's privacy options form.
  • Non-personalised ads: where you do not consent, only non-personalised advertising is served.

AdMob and its partners process data under Google's own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • Service providers: Google (AdMob) receives the advertising and device signals described above as part of showing ads.
  • Legal: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.
  • There is no other sharing, because we hold nothing to share.

7. Data Deletion

  • Uninstalling the app removes everything Nearpass stored on the device.
  • You can clear app data at any time from device settings, as described in section 3.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device's Google > Ads settings, which severs AdMob's link to your device.
  • You can withdraw personalised-ad consent inside the app at any time.
  • Because no personal data is sent to us, there is no server-side record for us to delete on request; if you still want to ask, use the contact below and we will confirm in writing that nothing about you is held.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children and is not marketed to children. It collects no personal data from anyone who uses it. Advertising is subject to Google Play policy and Google AdMob's audience restrictions.

9. Security

Transfers happen over links you control (your own Wi-Fi, a peer-to-peer Wi-Fi connection, or a hotspot one of the two phones creates). There is no user database to breach, and no Nearpass endpoint involved in a transfer. Ad-related communication happens only over encrypted HTTPS with Google's ad servers.

10. Changes to This Policy

If the app gains a feature that changes what data is processed — for example optional crash reporting or any cloud relay — this page is updated before that version is released.
