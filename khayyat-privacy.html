Privacy Policy — Khayyat Ledger (دفتر الخياط)

App: Khayyat Ledger (دفتر الخياط), Android — published by Desho.Co.Ltd

Package: com.desho.khayyat

Effective Date: October 2026

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

1. What this app is

Khayyat Ledger is the account book of a tailoring shop: customers and suppliers, invoices, statements of account, credit and instalments, stock, expenses and reports. It is written for Arabic-speaking shops and it works without an internet connection.

The short version of this policy is that the shop's book stays on the shop's phone. There is no Khayyat Ledger server, no account to register, and nothing that reads your customers out of the device.

2. What is not collected

  • No name, email, phone number, address or account is requested to use the app.
  • No analytics, no usage profiling, no remote crash reporting.
  • No advertising SDK is present in this version, so no ad identifier is collected.
  • No contacts, calendar, camera, microphone, call-log or location access is requested. The app declares no permission to read your phone's address book: customers are typed in by the shop.
  • Nothing is sold or rented to anyone.

Why the app still holds an internet permission

The build carries INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE because the app framework and Google Play's billing library are compiled with them, and com.android.vending.BILLING is declared for the subscription that is sold through Google Play. No feature of the ledger itself contacts a server: the app's own code has no URL, no client and no telemetry endpoint, and it completes every task, including printing, with the phone offline.

3. What is stored, and where

  • The ledger: customers, suppliers, orders, measurements, invoices and their lines, payments and instalments, stock items and movements, expenses, income and settings. It is one SQLite database in the app's private storage on this device, readable by no other app.
  • The lock: your PIN is kept as a salted PBKDF2 hash (210,000 rounds), never as the digits themselves. Fingerprint unlock uses the Android biometric system and the device's own key storage; a fingerprint image is never seen, taken or stored by this app.
  • Backups you ask for: the app writes two files — a readable JSON export and a copy of the database — into the folder you choose on this device, and keeps three dated copies. They are yours: the app does not upload them anywhere, and you can delete them at any time.
  • Paper you ask for: an invoice is rendered to a PDF on the device. Opening it, printing it or sharing it goes through Android's own print and share sheets, so the copy reaches only the printer or the app you pick in that moment.

Everything above is removed when you uninstall the app. You can clear it sooner: Settings > Apps > دفتر الخياط > Storage > Clear data.

4. Subscriptions and payment

The yearly subscription is sold by Google Play with Google Play Billing. The purchase token, the price and the entitlement are handled by Google under its own privacy policy; the app asks Play whether the subscription is active and nothing more. No card number, no payment profile and no Google account detail is collected or stored by this app. You can manage or cancel the subscription in Play Store > Payments & subscriptions.

In the version now under testing the paywall is not in the build yet, so no purchase is offered and nothing is charged: the billing permission is declared ahead of the release that uses it. When that release ships, this paragraph is where the change is recorded.

5. Children

This is a shop's account book for adult use. It is not directed at children and collects no data about them.

6. Your data, your control

Because the data never leaves the device, correction and deletion happen in the app itself: every record can be edited, and an invoice can be reversed or deleted within the rules the ledger enforces. Restoring an older backup replaces the current book with that copy. Uninstalling the app deletes the book and the lock.

7. If this policy changes

A new version of this page is posted here with a new "Last Updated" date before any release that changes what the app does with data. Nothing in an earlier version is kept in force by silence.
