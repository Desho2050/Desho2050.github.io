Privacy Policy — Medo The Gladiator

App: Medo The Gladiator (Android) — published by Desho.Co.Ltd

Effective Date: October 2026

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Medo The Gladiator is an offline single-player bridge battle runner. You steer one growing legion across a bridge of numbered gates and break the wall at the end of each stage. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

The game itself needs no account, no login and no server. Your progress, best legion size and settings are stored only on your device.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data stored on your device

  • Game progress: stage unlocks, upgrades, war bonuses and the legion count you reached. This never leaves your device.
  • Preferences: language (English or Arabic) and sound settings, stored locally.

2.2 Advertising data (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Advertising is optional to play: interstitial ads appear between stages, and a rewarded ad is offered only if you choose to bring your legion back once after a defeat.

  • Advertising ID (AAID): AdMob may use your device advertising identifier and device information to serve and measure ads.
  • Ad interactions: impressions, clicks and video completions may be recorded by AdMob and its network of advertisers.
  • Consent: before ads are shown, the app may ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform, depending on your region. You can withdraw or change that consent at any time from the app menu ("Ad privacy settings"), which opens Google's privacy options form.
  • Non-personalised ads: where you do not consent, or where the app is not directed at children, only non-personalised advertising is served.

AdMob and its partners may process data under Google's own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No name, email, phone number, address or account is requested or stored.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or call log access.
  • No gameplay analytics, no remote leaderboards, no profiling of your play.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

4. How Information Is Used

The only third-party processing in this app is advertising delivery and measurement by Google AdMob, so the game can remain free and playable offline. Data stored on your device is used only to continue your own progress.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • Service providers: Google (AdMob) receives the advertising and device signals described above as part of showing ads.
  • Legal: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.
  • There is no other sharing, because there is nothing else to share.

6. Data Storage, Retention and Deletion

  • Your game data lives in the app's private storage on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • You can clear your progress at any time from your device: Settings > Apps > Medo The Gladiator > Storage > Clear data.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device's Google > Ads settings, which severs AdMob's link to your device.
  • You can withdraw personalised-ad consent in the app menu at any time.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and is not marketed to children. It collects no personal data from anyone who uses it. Advertising is subject to Google Play policy and Google AdMob's own audience restrictions.

8. Security

Because gameplay data stays on your device and the app performs no account authentication, there is no user database to breach. Ad-related communication happens only over encrypted HTTPS with Google's ad servers.

9. Changes to This Policy

If the app gains a feature that changes what data is processed, we will update this page and the date at the top before releasing that version.
